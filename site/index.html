See the practice loop View source

One calm, local loop.

SteadyTap focuses on the interaction itself: measure, adjust, compare, repeat.

01 / Calibrate

Tap and drag naturally

A short guided sequence measures target distance, touch variation, drag deviation, and timing on the device.

02 / Compare

Run baseline and adaptive rounds

The app adjusts target scale, spacing, hold duration, and swipe thresholds from the local calibration.

03 / Review

See what changed

Results compare accuracy, misses, time, and score change, then save a compact local summary.

Private by design.

The App Store release stays useful without an account or a server connection.

No account

Open the app and begin. There is no sign-in, profile creation, or external hardware requirement.

No tracking

No advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or cross-app identifier is included in version 1.0.

Your controls

Clear Local Data resets saved sessions and preferences. Removing the app deletes its local container.

Touch practice, clearly bounded.

SteadyTap is an interface-calibration and practice utility. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, monitor, prevent, or treat any condition.
